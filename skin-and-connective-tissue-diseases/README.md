**分类定义 / Definition**：皮肤与结缔组织疾病涵盖影响皮肤、附属器及皮下结缔组织的广泛疾病谱，包括自身免疫性、遗传性、感染性与肿瘤性疾病。其核心病理机制涉及免疫失调、胶原沉积和慢性炎症，常导致皮肤结构异常、纤维化或多系统损害。代表性病种如系统性红斑狼疮、硬皮病及银屑病。临床特征多样，从皮肤硬结、红斑、光敏到内脏受累，诊断整合病理学、血清标志物与影像学。研究前沿聚焦于疾病易感基因、新型生物制剂及干细胞疗法，推动精准分型与靶向治疗。多学科协作在皮肤科、风湿免疫科及病理科间尤为重要，以优化长期管理策略。

*Skin and Connective Tissue Diseases represent a broad spectrum of disorders affecting the skin, dermal appendages, and subcutis. These encompass autoimmune, hereditary, infectious, and malignant conditions, with common pathomechanisms such as immune dysregulation, aberrant collagen deposition, and persistent inflammation. This leads to impaired skin barrier function, fibrosis, or multisystem involvement. Notable examples include systemic lupus erythematosus, systemic sclerosis, and psoriasis. Clinical presentation varies widely, featuring sclerotic changes, erythematous plaques, photosensitivity, and potential organ damage. Diagnostic frameworks integrate dermatopathology, autoantibody profiling, and advanced imaging. Current research emphasizes disease susceptibility genes, novel biologic agents, and regenerative medicine strategies, paving the way for molecular subtyping and tailored therapies. Optimal management demands interdisciplinary cooperation between dermatology, rheumatology, pathology, and other specialties to mitigate disease burden.*
