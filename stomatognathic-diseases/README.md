**分类定义 / Definition**：口颌系统疾病是指累及口腔、颌骨、咀嚼肌、颞下颌关节及相关神经血管的一类复杂疾病群，涵盖牙齿硬组织、牙周支持组织、口腔黏膜、唾液腺、颌骨及颅面结构的功能障碍与器质性病变。其发病机制常涉及遗传、微生物感染、免疫异常、后天不良习惯、创伤及神经精神因素等多重交互作用。临床上可表现为疼痛、咀嚼功能受限、咬合紊乱、颌面畸形、吞咽或言语障碍以及口腔软组织损伤，严重者可导致营养障碍和社交心理问题。随着口腔医学与多学科交叉的推进，口颌系统疾病的研究重点已从传统形态学修复转向生物力学调控、神经肌肉协调、再生修复及数字化精准诊疗，在医学数据平台与临床知识库中，对该类疾病的系统分类与知识图谱构建对于辅助决策、流行病学分析和个体化治疗具有重要价值。

*Stomatognathic diseases encompass a broad spectrum of disorders affecting the oral cavity, jaws, masticatory muscles, temporomandibular joints, and associated neurovascular structures. These conditions involve hard dental tissues, periodontal support, oral mucosa, salivary glands, and craniofacial structures, manifesting as functional impairments or organic lesions. Etiopathogenesis is multifactorial, integrating genetic predisposition, microbial infection, immune dysregulation, parafunctional habits, trauma, and neuropsychological factors. Common clinical presentations include orofacial pain, masticatory dysfunction, occlusal disharmony, dentofacial deformities, dysphagia, speech difficulties, and oral soft tissue lesions, which may lead to nutritional compromise and psychosocial distress. In modern medical data platforms and clinical knowledge bases, systematic classification and knowledge graph construction of stomatognathic diseases are pivotal for decision support, epidemiological research, and personalized treatment strategies, with emerging focus on biomechanical modulation, neuromuscular coordination, regenerative repair, and digital precision medicine.*
