**分类定义 / Definition**：呼吸道疾病涵盖影响从鼻腔至肺泡整个气体传导与交换通道的各类疾患，包括感染性、炎症性、肿瘤性、结构性及免疫介导性病变。其发病机制涉及病原体侵袭、环境暴露、遗传易感及气道高反应性等多种因素，常表现为咳嗽、气促、胸闷及呼吸功能障碍。临床管理依赖病原学检测、肺功能评估、影像学及内镜技术，重点在于抗感染、抗炎、气道管理与呼吸支持。随着空气污染加重与新发病原体出现，呼吸道疾病已成为全球重大公共卫生负担，推动精准诊断、靶向治疗和疫苗研发成为关键方向。

*Respiratory tract diseases encompass a broad spectrum of conditions affecting the conducting airways and gas exchange surfaces from the nasal passages to the alveoli, including infectious, inflammatory, neoplastic, structural, and immune-mediated disorders. Pathogenesis involves microbial invasion, environmental exposures, genetic susceptibility, and airway hyperresponsiveness, frequently presenting with cough, dyspnea, chest tightness, and impaired respiratory function. Clinical management relies on microbiological diagnosis, pulmonary function testing, imaging, and endoscopic techniques, with emphasis on anti-infective therapy, anti-inflammatory strategies, airway clearance, and ventilatory support. As a leading contributor to global morbidity and mortality, respiratory diseases drive ongoing research into precision diagnostics, targeted therapeutics, and vaccine development amid the challenges of air pollution and emerging pathogens.*
