**分类定义 / Definition**：血液与淋巴系统疾病涵盖血液、骨髓、淋巴结及脾脏等造血与免疫器官的结构与功能异常，主要包括红细胞、白细胞、血小板的数量或质量缺陷，以及淋巴组织的增生或恶性转化。其病理机制涉及基因突变、免疫失调、营养缺乏及感染等，可导致贫血、出血、血栓、免疫功能紊乱及器官浸润。临床表现为乏力、发热、淋巴结肿大、出血倾向等，诊断依赖血液学、骨髓检查及免疫分型。近年来，靶向治疗、细胞免疫治疗及造血干细胞移植显著改善了预后，基因组学与精准医疗的融入为疾病分层和个体化治疗提供了新方向，推动血液病管理向更精准、综合的模式发展。

*Hemic and lymphatic diseases encompass a broad spectrum of disorders affecting the blood, bone marrow, lymph nodes, and spleen, involving abnormalities in the production, function, or malignant transformation of erythrocytes, leukocytes, platelets, and lymphoid tissues. Pathogenic mechanisms include genetic mutations, immune dysregulation, nutritional deficiencies, and infections, leading to manifestations such as anemia, bleeding diatheses, thrombosis, immunodeficiency, and tissue infiltration. Clinical presentations range from fatigue and fever to lymphadenopathy and petechiae, with diagnosis relying on hematological tests, bone marrow examination, and immunophenotyping. Advances in targeted therapies, cellular immunotherapy, and hematopoietic stem cell transplantation have markedly improved outcomes. The integration of genomics and precision medicine enables refined risk stratification and individualized treatment, steering disease management toward more accurate and integrative paradigms.*
