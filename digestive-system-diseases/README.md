**分类定义 / Definition**：消化系统疾病涵盖从食管到肛门的消化道、肝、胆、胰及腹膜等器官的结构与功能异常，是临床最常见的疾病群之一。其病因多样，涉及感染、免疫紊乱、代谢障碍、肿瘤、血管病变及精神心理因素等，病理机制常包括黏膜损伤、分泌与动力失调、菌群失衡及恶性转化。主要临床表现有吞咽困难、腹痛、腹泻、便秘、黄疸及消化道出血等。消化内镜、影像学及分子诊断技术的发展显著提升了早期病变的检出与精准分期。这类疾病的研究聚焦于肠-脑轴调控、微生态与免疫交互、早癌筛查策略及多学科诊疗模式，是基础研究与转化医学的重要领域。

*Digestive system diseases encompass a broad range of disorders affecting the alimentary tract from the esophagus to the anus, as well as the liver, biliary system, pancreas, and peritoneum. They are among the most prevalent conditions worldwide, arising from diverse etiologies including infections, autoimmune dysregulation, metabolic disturbances, neoplasms, vascular compromise, and psychosocial factors. Core pathophysiological mechanisms involve mucosal injury, disordered motility and secretion, gut microbiota imbalance, and malignant transformation. Common clinical presentations include dysphagia, abdominal pain, diarrhea, constipation, jaundice, and gastrointestinal bleeding. Advances in endoscopy, imaging, and molecular diagnostics have substantially improved early detection and accurate staging. Research in this field focuses on gut–brain axis modulation, microbe–host interactions, early cancer screening strategies, and multidisciplinary management, positioning it as a vital area in both fundamental science and translational medicine.*
