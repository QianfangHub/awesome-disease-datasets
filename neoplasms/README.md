**分类定义 / Definition**：肿瘤是一类以细胞异常增殖为特征的疾病，可在全身各组织器官发生，分为良性、恶性和交界性三类。恶性肿瘤（即癌症）具有侵袭和转移能力，是全球主要的疾病负担之一。其发生机制涉及基因突变、表观遗传改变、信号通路异常及微环境重塑，常表现为失控性生长、组织浸润和远处扩散。临床诊疗依赖于影像学、病理活检和分子分型，治疗手段包括手术、放疗、化疗、靶向及免疫治疗等。肿瘤学的研究聚焦于基因组不稳定性、肿瘤异质性、耐药机制和早期筛查标志物，推动精准医学和个体化治疗的发展。无论在临床实践还是公共卫生层面，肿瘤的预防、早诊和综合管理始终是核心议题。

*Neoplasms are diseases characterized by abnormal cell proliferation that can arise in virtually any tissue or organ, classified as benign, malignant, or borderline. Malignant neoplasms (cancers) possess the ability to invade and metastasize, representing a major global disease burden. Their pathogenesis involves genetic mutations, epigenetic alterations, dysregulated signaling pathways, and microenvironmental remodeling, leading to uncontrolled growth, local invasion, and distant spread. Clinical diagnosis relies on imaging, pathological biopsy, and molecular subtyping, while treatment encompasses surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, targeted therapy, and immunotherapy. Oncological research focuses on genomic instability, tumor heterogeneity, drug resistance mechanisms, and early detection biomarkers, driving advances in precision medicine and personalized care. Across both clinical and public health domains, prevention, early diagnosis, and integrated management of neoplasms remain central priorities.*
