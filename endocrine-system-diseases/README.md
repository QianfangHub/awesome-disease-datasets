**分类定义 / Definition**：内分泌系统疾病是内分泌腺体或靶组织功能异常导致激素分泌、调控或作用障碍的一类疾病，涵盖下丘脑-垂体-甲状腺轴、肾上腺、性腺、胰岛及弥散神经内分泌系统等。其核心病理机制包括激素过量或不足、受体及信号转导缺陷、内分泌腺体增生或肿瘤、自身免疫破坏及遗传性激素合成酶缺陷。临床上常表现为代谢紊乱、生长发育异常、生殖功能障碍及电解质失衡等多系统受累，症状隐匿且个体差异显著。近年来，随着分子内分泌学、基因组学及靶向治疗的发展，该类疾病的诊断正从生化指标向精准分型转变，研究方向集中于神经内分泌调控、肠道激素、内分泌干扰物及免疫检查点抑制剂相关内分泌不良事件等领域。

*Endocrine system diseases encompass disorders arising from dysfunction of endocrine glands or target tissues, leading to abnormalities in hormone secretion, regulation, or action. These conditions span the hypothalamic-pituitary-thyroid axis, adrenal glands, gonads, pancreatic islets, and the diffuse neuroendocrine system. Core pathological mechanisms include hormone excess or deficiency, receptor and signaling defects, glandular hyperplasia or neoplasia, autoimmune destruction, and inherited enzymatic defects in hormone synthesis. Clinical manifestations are often multisystemic and insidious, characterized by metabolic disturbances, growth and developmental abnormalities, reproductive dysfunction, and electrolyte imbalances, with considerable individual variability. Advances in molecular endocrinology, genomics, and targeted therapies are shifting diagnosis from biochemical markers toward precision classification. Current research focuses on neuroendocrine regulation, gut hormones, endocrine-disrupting chemicals, and immune checkpoint inhibitor-related endocrine adverse events.*
