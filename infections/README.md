**分类定义 / Definition**：感染性疾病是由病原微生物（如细菌、病毒、真菌、寄生虫等）侵入人体并繁殖所引发的一类疾病，可累及全身各组织器官。其病理机制涉及病原体致病因子与宿主免疫应答的相互作用，导致局部炎症、组织损伤或全身性反应。临床特点包括发热、炎症指标升高及器官特异性功能障碍，部分感染具有传染性，可通过呼吸道、消化道、血液等途径在人群中传播。在医学研究与临床管理中，感染性疾病是重要领域，涵盖流行病学监测、病原体鉴定、抗微生物治疗、疫苗研发及医院感染控制等方面，对全球公共卫生构成持续挑战。

*Infectious diseases are disorders caused by pathogenic microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, and parasites, which invade the body and multiply, potentially affecting any organ system. Their pathogenesis involves interactions between pathogen virulence factors and host immune responses, leading to localized inflammation, tissue damage, or systemic manifestations. Clinical features commonly include fever, elevated inflammatory markers, and organ-specific dysfunction, with certain infections being transmissible through respiratory, gastrointestinal, or bloodborne routes. In medical research and practice, infectious diseases represent a critical domain encompassing epidemiological surveillance, pathogen identification, antimicrobial therapy, vaccine development, and healthcare-associated infection control, posing ongoing challenges to global public health.*
