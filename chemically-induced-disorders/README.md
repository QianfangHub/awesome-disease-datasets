**分类定义 / Definition**：化学诱发性障碍是指因暴露于外源性化学物质或药物，导致机体器官、系统在结构或功能上发生异常的一组疾病。其致病机制涉及化学物对细胞的直接毒性、免疫介导损伤、代谢激活或遗传易感性等因素，临床表现多样且与接触剂量、途径及个体差异密切相关。涵盖范围包括化学物引起的血液系统损害、呼吸系统疾病、肝肾损伤、神经系统毒性及药物相关不良反应等。近年来，随着环境暴露组学与药物安全监测体系的完善，化学诱发性障碍的研究重点逐渐转向早期生物标志物筛查、毒理基因组学评估及个体化风险预测，为临床精准防治提供了重要支持。

*Chemically-induced disorders refer to a group of diseases resulting from exposure to exogenous chemical substances or pharmaceuticals, leading to structural or functional abnormalities in various organs and systems. The underlying mechanisms involve direct cytotoxicity, immune-mediated injury, metabolic activation, and genetic susceptibility, with clinical manifestations heavily influenced by dose, route, and individual vulnerability. This category encompasses conditions such as blood dyscrasias, respiratory illnesses, hepatic and renal toxicities, neurotoxicity, and adverse drug reactions. Advances in environmental exposomics and pharmacovigilance have shifted research focus toward early biomarker identification, toxicogenomic profiling, and personalized risk assessment, thereby enhancing precision in clinical prevention and management.*
