**分类定义 / Definition**：泌尿生殖系统疾病涵盖泌尿系统与生殖系统的器质性及功能性疾病，两者在胚胎发育、解剖结构及生理功能上密切关联。该领域涉及肾脏、输尿管、膀胱、尿道以及男女性内、外生殖器官发生的感染、结石、肿瘤、先天性畸形、内分泌与代谢紊乱、免疫损伤及神经调控异常等病变。病理机制常表现为肾小球滤过屏障破坏、尿路梗阻、性激素轴失衡、盆底支持结构缺陷等，临床以排尿障碍、疼痛、肿块、异常出血、不育等为主要表现。在现代医学中，泌尿生殖系统疾病的研究聚焦于精准诊断、微创手术与腔内治疗、器官功能重建及再生医学，同时强调多学科协作管理，对保障患者生命质量与生殖健康具有重要意义。

*Urogenital diseases encompass a broad spectrum of structural and functional disorders affecting the urinary and reproductive systems, which share close embryological, anatomical, and physiological interconnections. This category includes conditions such as glomerulonephritis, urolithiasis, and benign prostatic hyperplasia, as well as infectious, neoplastic, congenital, endocrine, and neurogenic pathologies of the kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra, and genital organs. Pathogenetic mechanisms involve disruptions in glomerular filtration barrier, urinary tract obstruction, sex hormone axis dysregulation, and pelvic floor support deficits, leading to clinical manifestations like voiding dysfunction, pain, masses, abnormal bleeding, and infertility. The field emphasizes early detection through biomarkers, minimally invasive and robotic surgery, organ reconstruction, and regenerative therapies. Integrating multi-disciplinary care is essential to improving both survival and quality of life, driving translational research and personalized treatment strategies in urogenital medicine.*
