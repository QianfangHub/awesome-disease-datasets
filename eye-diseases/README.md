**分类定义 / Definition**：眼病泛指影响视觉系统结构与功能的各类疾病，涵盖眼球、视神经、眼附属器及视觉通路等多个层面。其病理机制复杂多样，包括炎症、感染、变性、血管异常、肿瘤、外伤及遗传因素等。常见临床表现如视力下降、视物变形、眼痛、眼红及视野缺损，严重影响患者的生活质量。随着人口老龄化及生活方式改变，年龄相关性黄斑变性、糖尿病视网膜病变及青光眼等致盲性眼病的负担日益加重。精准诊断依赖于裂隙灯检查、眼底成像、光学相干断层扫描及眼压测量等多模态技术，治疗手段涵盖药物、激光、手术及基因疗法。临床研究与数据平台建设正致力于眼病的早期筛查、风险预测和个体化干预，为防盲治盲提供科学支撑。

*Eye diseases encompass a broad spectrum of disorders affecting the structure and function of the visual system, including the eyeball, optic nerve, ocular adnexa, and visual pathways. The underlying pathologies are diverse, involving inflammation, infection, degeneration, vascular abnormalities, neoplasms, trauma, and genetic factors. Common clinical presentations such as vision loss, distorted vision, ocular pain, redness, and visual field defects can profoundly impact quality of life. With population aging and changing lifestyles, the burden of blinding conditions like age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and glaucoma continues to rise. Accurate diagnosis relies on multimodal technologies including slit-lamp examination, fundus imaging, optical coherence tomography, and tonometry, while treatment options range from medications and laser therapy to surgery and gene-based approaches. Ongoing clinical research and data platform development aim to enhance early detection, risk stratification, and personalized management, supporting global efforts in blindness prevention and eye health.*
