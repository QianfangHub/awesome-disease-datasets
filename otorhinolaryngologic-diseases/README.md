**分类定义 / Definition**：耳鼻咽喉疾病是累及耳部、鼻腔鼻窦、咽部、喉部及头颈部相关结构的一类临床常见疾病。其涵盖范围从急慢性炎症、过敏性与免疫性疾病，到良性及恶性肿瘤、先天畸形、外伤、神经性及功能性障碍等。病理机制多涉及感染、免疫失调、环境刺激、遗传及退行性改变。临床突出表现为听力下降、耳鸣、眩晕、鼻塞、嗅觉减退、声音嘶哑、吞咽困难及颈部肿块。随着内镜微创技术、人工听觉植入及靶向免疫治疗的进步，该领域的精准诊疗能力持续提升，同时，头颈肿瘤的多学科综合治疗与听觉言语康复已成为重点发展方向。

*Otorhinolaryngologic diseases encompass a broad spectrum of conditions affecting the ear, nose, paranasal sinuses, pharynx, larynx, and related head and neck structures. They range from acute and chronic infections, allergic and immunologic disorders, to benign and malignant neoplasms, congenital anomalies, trauma, and sensorineural impairments. Pathogenesis often involves infections, immune dysregulation, environmental irritants, genetic predispositions, and degenerative processes. Common clinical manifestations include hearing loss, tinnitus, vertigo, nasal obstruction, anosmia, dysphonia, dysphagia, and cervical masses. Advances in endoscopic surgery, cochlear implantation, and targeted immunotherapy have significantly enhanced diagnostic precision and treatment outcomes. Multidisciplinary management of head and neck cancer, alongside aural and speech rehabilitation, remains a major focus in contemporary otorhinolaryngology.*
