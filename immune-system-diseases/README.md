**分类定义 / Definition**：免疫系统疾病涵盖因免疫系统功能异常导致的一系列健康问题，涉及免疫防御过度、免疫监视缺失或免疫自稳失衡等机制。这类疾病可表现为自身免疫病、免疫缺陷病或超敏反应性疾病，例如类风湿关节炎、系统性红斑狼疮等。其病理核心在于免疫细胞或分子对自身抗原的错误识别，或对外来病原体的应答失调，造成组织损伤与炎症。临床上，免疫系统疾病常呈慢性、多系统受累的特点，症状复杂多样，从关节疼痛、皮疹到多器官功能障碍。精准诊断依赖免疫学检测、遗传分析与影像学手段，治疗策略包括免疫抑制剂、生物制剂和靶向药物。当前研究聚焦于免疫调节通路的分子机制、微生物组与免疫互作，以及个体化免疫治疗方案的开发。

*Immune system diseases refer to a spectrum of disorders arising from dysregulation of immune responses, including overactivity, deficiency, or misdirected attacks against the body's own tissues. Key categories include autoimmune conditions, immunodeficiencies, and hypersensitivity reactions, with diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis and systemic lupus erythematosus as representative examples. The underlying mechanisms involve abnormal activation of immune cells and cytokines, leading to chronic inflammation and tissue damage. Clinically, these diseases often manifest with multi-system involvement, variable symptoms, and a progressive course, posing challenges in diagnosis and management. Advances in immunology have spurred the development of targeted therapies, such as biologic agents and immune checkpoint modulators, fundamentally improving patient outcomes. Ongoing research emphasizes the role of genetic susceptibility, environmental triggers, and the microbiome in disease pathogenesis, paving the way for precision medicine in immune-related conditions.*
